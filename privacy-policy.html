1. Introduction & Scope

This document complies with global data privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) / California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Mandatory Google Play Accessibility Service API Disclosure

Skrollify utilizes Android's AccessibilityService API under Google Play's strict policy guidelines. To ensure total transparency, please review this explicit disclosure:

  • Why Accessibility is Used: Skrollify's Accessibility Service is activated solely when you enter supported short-form video applications (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Facebook Reels, and Snapchat Spotlight). It inspects layout structure in volatile device memory to:
    1. Accurately detect transitions to count how many short videos you watch in real time.
    2. Display the floating counter HUD and your emotional companion avatar over the video feed so you remain mindful of time.
    3. Execute user-configured digital wellbeing boundaries (e.g. Smart Break reminders or Auto-Exit redirecting you back to your home screen when your daily limit is hit).
  • Strict Boundaries (What Accessibility NEVER Does):
    • ❌ NO Keystroke Logging: We do NOT read, monitor, or record keystrokes, passwords, PINs, credit card numbers, or search queries.
    • ❌ NO Chat or Message Inspection: We do NOT access private messages, direct chats, comments, emails, or personal contacts.
    • ❌ NO Screen Recording or Audio Capture: We do NOT record your screen, take screenshots, capture camera video, or record microphone audio.
    • ❌ NO Remote Transmission of Layout Data: UI layout data is evaluated strictly in-memory on your phone and is immediately discarded. It is NEVER sent over the internet.
  • Prominent Consent: In accordance with Google Play rules, this permission is optional and only requested after you review an explicit prominent disclosure in the app. You can grant, revoke, or disable the service at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility.

4. Android Permissions Required & Justification

  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: Required to read foreground screen time durations through Android's official UsageStats API for monitored social apps and screen-time-only apps (WhatsApp, Reddit, LinkedIn).
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW ("Display over other apps"): Allows Skrollify to draw the lightweight floating counter HUD and companion avatar over active short-video feeds. The overlay auto-dismisses immediately when you leave monitored apps.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: (Android 13+) Used only to deliver daily milestone recaps, limit warnings, or friend group leaderboard alerts. You can silence notifications anytime.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Optional; requested so vendor battery managers (Samsung One UI, Xiaomi MIUI) do not kill the floating counter while you are actively tracking reels.

6. European Privacy Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): You can request a copy of the personal data associated with your cloud account.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): You can update your display name, email, or avatar anytime in App Settings.
  • Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Art. 17): You can permanently delete your account and all associated Firestore data directly in App Settings > Account > Delete Account, or by emailing us.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): You can export your local usage logs and SQLite data via the in-app export feature.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7): You can sign out, disable Accessibility, or turn off group sync at any time without penalty.

9. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

  • Local Data: Stored on your device until you clear app data, uninstall Skrollify, or tap "Reset Data" in settings.
  • Cloud Data: Retained in Firebase Firestore as long as your account remains active.
  • Immediate Deletion: When you tap "Delete Account" in settings or send an email request, all associated cloud records (user profile, group memberships, and leaderboard scores) are permanently erased within 48 hours.

Local databases are sandbox-isolated within Android's private app storage (/data/data/com.ayanhaider.skrollify/), inaccessible to other apps on non-rooted devices.
