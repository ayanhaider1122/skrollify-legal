# Skrollify — Screen Specifications

## Screen 1: Onboarding 1 — Welcome ("Your journey begins")
* **Header**: Glowing leaf emblem (`🍃` with radial gradient ambient aura)
* **Title**: "Welcome to\nTK Decay" (34.sp ExtraBold, warm ivory)
* **Subtitle**: "Your digital companion\nfor a healthier mind." (16.sp, text muted)
* **Center**: 3D chubby Sproutling avatar sitting on ground with subtle radial spotlight
* **Primary Button**: "Get Started" (Ivory pill button, 56.dp height)
* **Secondary Action**: "I already have an account" (13.sp text muted)

## Screen 2: Onboarding 2 — Choose Apps ("Where scrolling happens")
* **Title**: "Which apps do you\nuse the most?" (28.sp ExtraBold)
* **Subtitle**: "Select the apps you spend the most time on." (14.sp)
* **List Items**:
  1. Instagram (Official gradient camera badge)
  2. TikTok (Official musical note badge)
  3. YouTube (Official red play badge)
  4. Snapchat (Official yellow ghost badge)
  5. WhatsApp (Official green phone bubble badge)
  6. Other (Clean blue badge)
* **Checkbox**: Right-aligned circular indicator (solid white with checkmark when selected, subtle dark outline when unselected)
* **Action**: "Next" button

## Screen 3: Onboarding 3 — Set Baseline ("Honest reflection")
* **Title**: "How much time do you\nusually spend?" (28.sp ExtraBold)
* **Subtitle**: "Be honest — it helps us give you a better plan." (14.sp)
* **Options**:
  - `< 30 minutes`
  - `30 – 60 minutes`
  - `1 – 2 hours` (default selected)
  - `2 – 4 hours`
  - `4+ hours`
* **Radio Indicator**: Left-aligned circular radio button (white filled inner dot on selection)
* **Action**: "Next" button

## Screen 4: Onboarding 4 — Set Intention ("What matters most")
* **Title**: "What's your goal?" (28.sp ExtraBold)
* **Subtitle**: "How would you like to improve?" (14.sp)
* **Options**:
  - `Scroll less`
  - `Be more focused`
  - `Sleep better`
  - `Feel happier` (default selected)
  - `All of the above`
* **Radio Indicator**: Left-aligned circular radio button
* **Action**: "Next" button

## Screen 5: Onboarding 5 — Meet Your Companion ("Pick your avatar")
* **Title**: "Pick your avatar" (28.sp ExtraBold)
* **Subtitle**: "Your companion will grow with you." (14.sp)
* **Carousel Area**:
  - Left navigation chevron `‹`
  - 3D Chubby Sproutling hero asset in ambient spotlight
  - Right navigation chevron `›`
* **Palette Dots**: 5 circular color swatches below the mascot (Sage, Cream, Matcha Olive, Blossom Pink, Slate Blue)
* **Action**: "Continue" button

## Screen 6: Onboarding 6 — Allow Tracking ("Almost there!")
* **Title**: "Almost there!" (28.sp ExtraBold)
* **Subtitle**: "Enable usage access to\ntrack your activity." (15.sp)
* **Feature Card**: Dark container with 3 items:
  - 📱 See your app usage
  - 📊 Get accurate stats
  - 🔒 Keep your data private
* **Action**: "Enable" button (Ivory pill)
* **Secondary**: "Maybe later"

## Screen 7: Home Dashboard ("The heart of Skrollify")
* **Top Bar**: Leaf brand mark + streak pill (`🔥 4d`) + Settings gear
* **Condition Tag**: Elevated pill indicating current condition ("Thriving", "Healthy", "Tired", "Wilted")
* **Mascot Hero**: High-res Sproutling avatar responding to actual usage
* **Speech Bubble**: Encouraging dialogue from Sproutling
* **Today's Scrolling Card**:
  - "Today's Scrolling"
  - Large metrics: `42 / 120 min`
  - Smooth sage progress bar
  - Remaining time: `78 min remaining within your goal`
* **Monitored App Capsules**: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube with duration
* **Primary Action**: "Take a 5-minute reset" (Ivory pill button)
* **Bottom Navigation**: 4 clean tabs: Home, Friends, Progress, Profile
