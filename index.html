2. AccessibilityService API Disclosure (Mandatory Google Play Compliance)

To deliver real-time mindful scroll counting and automated feed break enforcement, Skrollify requires the Android AccessibilityService API (android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE). In accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, we declare the following with complete transparency:

  • Specific Purpose: The AccessibilityService is utilized exclusively to detect vertical scroll gestures, window transitions, and short-video container state changes within designated target applications (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Snapchat Spotlight, and Facebook Reels).
  • Strictly Local Event Evaluation: All accessibility event processing (evaluating view resource IDs, scroll positions, and window content changes) occurs entirely on your physical device in volatile system memory.
  • NO Surveillance or Media Capture: Skrollify does NOT record, stream, capture, or transmit screenshots, video feeds, camera images, or microphone audio.
  • NO Keystroke or Input Interception: The service does NOT observe, monitor, or record personal typing, passwords, messages, credit card numbers, or interactive input fields.
  • NO Remote Accessibility Data Exfiltration: Accessibility event metadata is never transmitted to our servers or third-party analytical platforms. It is processed in real time and immediately discarded after incrementing your local counter.
  • User-Controlled Permission: The Accessibility permission is entirely voluntary and must be explicitly enabled by you through Android System Settings. You can revoke it at any time.

4. Volatile Screen-Color Sampling for Tab Cover

To provide a seamless visual integration when the floating companion HUD is active over short-video players, the App may read a single pixel color sample from the active feed container. This color sample is strictly processed in temporary device RAM to adjust the HUD's border tone (light vs dark mode). It is never written to persistent storage, never converted to an image, and never transmitted off your device.

5. Optional Firebase Cloud Account & Friend Squads

Creating an account in Skrollify is completely optional. You can enjoy all core tracking, companion animations, and blocking features anonymously without registering.

If you choose to create an account (via Google Sign-In or Anonymous Auth):

  • We do NOT store your contacts, browsing history, or detailed timestamps of specific videos watched.
  • You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time directly in the app or via our Account Deletion Page.
