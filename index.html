Privacy Policy Highlights

Skrollify ("we", "our") is committed to protecting your personal privacy. Our Accessibility Service is activated solely when you enter supported short-form video viewers (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Facebook Reels, and Snapchat Spotlight). Layout tree structures are analyzed strictly in temporary device memory to increment the session reel counter and manage floating overlays.

We do not access personal communications, keystrokes, camera feeds, or contacts. Any optional profile data (such as your chosen avatar name and streak score) is stored securely on your local device.
