Refund Policy

Application: com.ayanhaider.skrollify Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

Thank you for supporting Skrollify. We are committed to complete transparency regarding in-app purchases and subscriptions. Because all purchases are processed through Google Play, refund requests are primarily handled via Google Play's standardized billing systems.

2. Google Play 48-Hour Automated Refund Window

If less than 48 hours have passed since your purchase or renewal, you can request a direct, automated refund directly through Google Play:

  1. Go to Google Play Order History on your browser or open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Find the Skrollify order you wish to refund.
  3. Select Request a refund or Report a problem.
  4. Complete the short form and indicate your reason. Google typically renders a decision within 15 minutes to 4 business days.

3. 14-Day Developer Refund Guarantee

If Google's 48-hour automated window has elapsed, we offer a voluntary 14-day direct developer refund guarantee for persistent technical defects, duplicate charges, or accidental billing renewals:

  • Send an email to support@skrollify.com with the subject Refund Request - Skrollify [Order ID].
  • Include your Google Play GPA order number (starts with GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx, found on your emailed Google Play receipt).
  • We will verify and issue the refund directly through the Google Play Developer Console within 24–48 business hours.

4. How to Cancel Subscriptions

Canceling a recurring subscription prevents all future billing while keeping your Pro access active through the end of the paid billing period:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  3. Select Skrollify and tap Cancel subscription.

5. Contact Us

For any billing inquiries, order verifications, or refund questions, email us at support@skrollify.com.
