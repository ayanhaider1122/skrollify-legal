Our Customer Guarantee: We want you to be 100% satisfied with Skrollify. Because all payments and subscriptions are handled through Google Play Billing, both Google's automated refund systems and our direct developer support are available to assist you.

1. General Refund Terms (Google Play Billing)

All in-app purchases, monthly subscriptions, annual subscriptions, and lifetime unlocks in Skrollify are processed through the Google Play Store. Consequently, transactions are governed by the Google Play Refund Policies and applicable local consumer protection legislation.

2. Immediate Refunds within 48 Hours (Fastest Method)

If less than 48 hours have passed since you made an in-app purchase or paid for a subscription:

How to request an instant refund through Google Play:
  1. Open a web browser and visit: Google Play Order History (or open the Google Play Help Center).
  2. Locate your Skrollify order.
  3. Select Request a refund or Report a problem.
  4. Choose the reason (e.g., "I purchased this by accident" or "Purchase not working as expected") and submit.

Google typically processes and approves eligible requests within minutes to 24 hours.

3. Developer Direct Refunds (After 48 Hours)

If more than 48 hours have passed and Google Play directs you to contact the developer, we are happy to assist you directly:

  • Eligibility: If you experienced a persistent technical defect that prevented Pro features from activating, or if you were charged mistakenly due to an unexpected renewal and reach out within 14 days, we can initiate a refund directly through the Google Play Developer Console.
  • Required Information: Send an email to ayanhaider.dev@gmail.com with:
    • Subject: Refund Request - Skrollify [Order ID]
    • Your Google Play Order Number (starts with GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx, found on your emailed Google receipt).
    • The Google account email associated with the purchase.
    • Brief description of the issue.

4. Subscription Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your recurring subscription (Monthly Support or Annual Pro) at any time. When you cancel:

  • Access Remains Active: You will continue to have full access to all Skrollify Pro features until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • No Future Charges: You will not be billed for any subsequent cycles after cancellation.
  • How to Cancel:
    1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android phone.
    2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner.
    3. Tap Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
    4. Tap Skrollify.
    5. Tap Cancel subscription and follow on-screen prompts.

5. European Union (EU / UK) Statutory Right of Withdrawal

Under the European Consumer Rights Directive (Directive 2011/83/EU) and equivalent UK legislation, consumers in the EU/EEA/UK generally have a 14-day statutory right of withdrawal for consumer contracts.

However, for digital content supplied immediately upon purchase (such as in-app unlocks and instant Pro activations), you acknowledge that by initiating digital performance upon checkout, you expressly consent to immediate access and acknowledge the forfeiture of the standard 14-day withdrawal right once digital supply begins. Nevertheless, our voluntary developer refund policy remains available as described above for technical defects or billing errors.

6. Accidental Purchases by Minors

If an unauthorized or accidental in-app purchase was made by a child on your device, you can request a refund directly through Google Play within 48 hours. To prevent future unauthorized purchases, we strongly recommend enabling Biometric / Password verification for all purchases in the Google Play Store app under Settings > Authentication > Require authentication for purchases.
