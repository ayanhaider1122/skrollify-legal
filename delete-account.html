Account & Data Deletion Instructions

Application: com.ayanhaider.skrollify Account & Data Deletion Policy

Your Data, Your Control

In compliance with Google Play Data Safety requirements and global privacy standards (GDPR / CCPA), Skrollify provides clear, immediate, and permanent mechanisms to delete your account and all associated cloud data.

1. Option 1: In-App Instant Account Deletion (Recommended)

If you created an optional cloud account in Skrollify, you can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from within the Android application:

  1. Open the Skrollify app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your Profile Avatar in the top navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Settings (Gear) icon in the top right corner.
  4. Scroll down to the Account & Security section.
  5. Tap Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  6. Confirm your decision when prompted.

Result: Your Firebase Authentication record, chosen display name, avatar configuration, squad memberships, and all cloud daily streak records are permanently and irreversibly purged from our database immediately.

2. Option 2: Web & Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your Android device, you can submit a manual deletion request by email:

  • Send an email to support@skrollify.com.
  • Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - Skrollify.
  • Provide the email address associated with your Google Sign-In or your Skrollify user ID.

We process all email deletion requests within 24 to 48 business hours and will send you a confirmation once your cloud records have been permanently erased.

3. Deleting Local On-Device Data

If you used Skrollify anonymously without creating a cloud account, all your scroll metrics and preferences reside exclusively on your physical phone. To permanently erase this local data:

  1. Open Android Settings on your phone.
  2. Go to Apps > Skrollify.
  3. Tap Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data).

Uninstalling the Skrollify application also immediately deletes the local sandbox database.

4. Data Retention Policy Post-Deletion

When an account deletion request is processed:

  • Cloud Account & Profile: Deleted immediately and irreversibly.
  • Daily Scroll History: Purged immediately from cloud Firestore.
  • Squad Memberships: Removed from all friend squads immediately.
  • Retained Data: ZERO user personal data is retained. Financial transaction records (GPA invoice numbers) are maintained solely within Google Play Console as required by tax and accounting regulations.

5. Contact Support

For any questions regarding account or data deletion, email our dedicated developer support at support@skrollify.com.
