# Skrollify — Product Specification

## 1. Product Identity
* **App Name**: Skrollify
* **Display / Brand Mark**: Skrollify
* **Tagline**: Less Scroll. More You.
* **Core Principle**: Digital mindfulness that is encouraging, non-judgmental, and companion-driven. Never make the user feel guilty or worthless for exceeding a limit.

## 2. Core User Experience
People spend hours endlessly scrolling short-form video feeds (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat Spotlight, etc.) without realizing how much time has slipped away.
Skrollify helps them regain conscious awareness of their attention.

A cute, living sprout companion (**Sproutling**) responds directly to their scrolling habits:
* **Mindful habits** keep the companion thriving, energetic, upright, and blooming.
* **Excessive scrolling** causes the companion to feel fatigued and wilt.
* **Mindful breaks & resets** help it recharge, recover energy, and unlock new appearances.

## 3. Product Principles
1. **Kindness Over Guilt**: No harsh alarms or punishing messages.
2. **True Visual Art Direction**: No generic Material shapes or cheap placeholders. High-fidelity original 3D mascot artwork with consistent lighting, organic proportions, and cinematic presence.
3. **Restrained Elegance**: Deep near-black background (`#0B0B09`), warm ivory typography (`#F4F0E6`), subtle borders (`#35352F`), and muted sage accents (`#C7D9AC`).
4. **Honest Tracking**: Direct integration with Android's `UsageStatsManager`, tracking real foreground application duration rather than guessing video swipes.
5. **Clear Separation of Local & Cloud**: Works 100% offline and locally out of the box with zero required accounts.
